Downbeat Privacy

Downbeat stores the takes you keep: the name, tempo, beats in a bar, the audio copy, how early or late each bar was, and which bars you marked to practice again. There is no account and no sign-in.

That data stays on this iPhone. Downbeat does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not show ads.

The microphone is used only after you tap Record with the click, so this take can be compared with the click. Opening a recording from Files does not use the microphone. Downbeat does not use the camera, photo library, location, or contacts.

Downbeat does not upload the recording and does not identify the song or the pitch.

Deleting a take, Delete all takes in About, or deleting the app, removes the takes stored by Downbeat.
