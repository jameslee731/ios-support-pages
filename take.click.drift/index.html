Downbeat

Downbeat lines a take up to a click and shows which bars landed early or late.

Tap Try a sample take on an empty home screen to hear a short scale. Bar 4 is late and bar 9 is early. Tap Hear on a bar to listen to that bar only.

To use your own playing, tap Record, name the take, and set the tempo and the number of beats in a bar. Open a recording from Files, or tap Record with the click and play along. Stop when the take is done.

Early sits above the line. Late sits below. A bar that will not line up says so, without a made-up number of milliseconds. Mark Practice again on a bar you want to redo. The mark stays until you turn it off.

Takes stay on this iPhone until you delete them. Delete all takes is in About. Downbeat does not tune pitch and does not upload the recording.
