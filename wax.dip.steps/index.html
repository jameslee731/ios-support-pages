Waxwrite

Waxwrite plans what to wax before each dye bath, then walks the egg one step at a time.

Design an egg

Open a sample egg or start a new one. Pick a division, paint the cells, and place a star, wheat, ladder, wave, pine, or cross. The bath list shows the order, and a short note when two colors would turn muddy.

Write

The writing screen shows one action at a time: wax, a brushed-on color, a timed bath, or melting the wax off. You can step back. Closing the app keeps the step and the bath timer.

Share

From the egg you have open, share the steps as a PDF or the egg as a picture.
