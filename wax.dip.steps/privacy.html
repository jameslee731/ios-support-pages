Privacy Policy

Waxwrite plans the wax and dye order for a decorated egg. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The eggs you design, the colors on each cell, and the step you have reached are stored on this iPhone. Waxwrite does not ask you to create an account.

Files you share

You can share a step sheet as a PDF, or the egg as a picture, through the share sheet. Saving the picture to your photo library happens only when you choose that. Waxwrite does not upload those files. The place they go is the app or person you choose.

What Waxwrite does not collect

Waxwrite does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
