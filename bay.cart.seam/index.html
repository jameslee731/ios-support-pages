Which book

Which book shows which book on this shelf is out of place, and the gap a book from the cart belongs in.

The shelf

Load the sample shelf, or add a book. A book that is out of place shows Out. Tap it to read which neighbor it should follow, and whether the volume or a decimal digit decides that. Move here puts it there. The shelf does not rearrange itself.

The cart

Open the cart for books that are not on the shelf yet. Tap one to see the gap. Close the cart and the gap goes away. Place it here inserts the book.

Dewey and Library of Congress

The brass label switches the comparison. Loading the Library of Congress sample does not remove your own shelf.
