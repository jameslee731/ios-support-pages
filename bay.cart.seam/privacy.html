Privacy Policy

Which book keeps the order of one shelf and marks the book that is in the wrong place. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The shelf, the call numbers, and the books still on the cart are stored on this iPhone. Which book does not ask you to create an account.

What Which book does not collect

Which book does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.
