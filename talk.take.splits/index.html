Talk Splits

Talk Splits helps you rehearse a timed talk section by section, see which part runs long and how much to cut, and replay just that part.

Tap New talk, give it a name and a time limit, and add a date if you have one. Then add sections. Each section has a name, a target time and a few lines of notes. The bar at the top of the talk shows whether your sections add up to the limit.

To rehearse, tap the round button at the bottom of the talk. After a short count-in, speak out loud and tap anywhere on the screen each time you move to the next section. The ring counts down the current section and turns red when it runs over. Undo takes back the last tap. Pause stops the clock and the recording. Finish ends the run early, and any sections you did not reach are marked Skipped.

After a run you see every section against its target: red runs over, green is on target, blue is short. If the whole run is over the limit, the Cut plan tells you how many seconds to take out of each section to land on time. Tap a section to hear just that part, at 1×, 1.25× or 1.5×. Touch and hold a section to share it as an audio file.

Compare all runs shows every rehearsal of the talk side by side, section by section, and marks the run closest to your limit.

The microphone is optional. If you choose Time only, or turn the microphone off in Settings, Talk Splits still times every section, but there is nothing to replay.

To edit a talk, use the button at the top right of the talk, or touch and hold it on the home screen. Swipe a section to delete it, or touch and hold to move it.

Try a sample talk on the empty home screen loads a wedding toast with two timed run-throughs.

Your talks and recordings stay on this iPhone. Delete all talks and recordings is in About.
