Privacy Policy

Talk Splits Privacy

Talk Splits stores your talks, their sections and notes, the times of each run-through and your run notes on this iPhone. There is no account and no sign-in.

If you allow the microphone, Talk Splits records each rehearsal so you can replay a single section. Recordings are saved on this iPhone only. They leave the phone only when you share a run or a section through the system share sheet, to the place you choose. If you choose Time only, nothing is recorded.

Talk Splits does not use your location, camera, photos or contacts. It does not turn speech into text.

Talk Splits does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not show ads.

Deleting a run or a talk removes its recordings. Using Delete all talks and recordings in About, or deleting the app, removes everything stored by Talk Splits.
