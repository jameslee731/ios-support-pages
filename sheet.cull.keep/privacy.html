Privacy Policy

Cull Sheet Privacy

Cull Sheet stores your rounds, the photos you add to them, your Keep / Maybe / Cut marks, the order of your keeps and any captions on this iPhone. There is no account and no sign-in.

Photos you add are copied into Cull Sheet. Your photo library is never changed, and Cull Sheet does not scan the library. Copies leave the phone only when you export a contact sheet through the system share sheet, to the place you choose.

Cull Sheet does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Cull Sheet does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not show ads.

Deleting a photo, a round, or Delete all rounds in About, or deleting the app, removes the copies stored by Cull Sheet.
