Cull Sheet

Cull Sheet helps you cull a shoot down to the photos you will deliver, number them in order, and export a contact sheet that matches the count you promised.

Tap New round, give it a name and the number of photos you agreed to deliver. Add a client name and a due date if you have them. Then add photos from your library. They are copied into Cull Sheet; the originals are not changed.

Open Cull to go through the set. Keep, Maybe and Cut sit at the bottom. Only Keep is numbered. The count at the top shows how many you still owe, or how many you have over the number you promised. Undo takes back the last mark.

Arrange keeps puts the numbered photos in the order they will appear on the sheet. Drag to reorder. You can type a short caption under each photo.

Build sheet previews the contact sheet. Choose 2 × 3, 3 × 4 or 4 × 5, Letter or A4. Export PDF or export one image per page. Sharing opens the system share sheet only after you tap it.

Try a sample round on the empty home screen loads a Saturday market assignment with twelve photos already marked, so you can export a sheet without picking from the library.

Photos, marks and order stay on this iPhone. Delete all rounds is in About.
