Privacy Policy

Tagsplit Privacy

Tagsplit stores your sales, households, item photos, prices, orders and payouts on this iPhone. There is no account and no sign-in.

If you take a photo of an item, Tagsplit asks for camera access and keeps a smaller copy of the photo with that item. If you choose a photo from your library, Tagsplit receives only the photo you pick.

Tag PDFs and payout summaries are created on this iPhone. They leave the phone only when you share them through the system share sheet, to the place you choose.

Tagsplit does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not show ads.

Deleting an item, deleting a sale, using Delete all sales in About, or deleting the app removes the sales and photos stored by Tagsplit.
