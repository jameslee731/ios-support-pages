Privacy Policy

Phase Belt places coil groups on a stator you are already rewinding. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The stators you create stay on this iPhone. That includes each group's phase, start slot, span, turns, wire size, and whether it is tucked, and the area of each slot. Phase Belt does not ask you to create an account.

What Phase Belt does not collect

Phase Belt does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
