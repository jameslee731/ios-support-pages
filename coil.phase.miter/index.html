Phase Belt

Phase Belt shows where the next coil group can start on a stator you are already rewinding. Finish stays off while a slot is over the fill limit, a group is still loose, or the three phases are not about 120° apart.

The end view

Load the 24-slot example, or start a new stator. Each number on the ring is a slot. Red means the copper does not fit. Drag a pale arc — a group you have not tucked — to another slot. A group already tucked stays where it is.

Finish

Where to tuck names a start slot that clears the tight window. Mark that group tucked, then tap Finish rewind. Last rewind shows the earlier slot areas and cannot be edited there.
