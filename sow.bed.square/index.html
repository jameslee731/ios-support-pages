Four by Eight

Four by Eight plans raised beds as one-foot squares, with one crop per square and every date counted from your frost dates.

Tap New bed and pick a size, or set columns and rows yourself. Each square is one foot, or 30 cm if you switch Square size in Settings.

To plant, tap a seed packet in the tray at the bottom of the bed, then tap empty squares. All crops lists every crop by family with how many fit in a square. Zucchini, winter squash and melons need a free 2 × 2 block. Tap a planted square to open it. Clear in the tray empties squares.

A red edge between squares means the two crops are better kept apart. A green dot means they grow well together. Open either square to read why. If you want a pair anyway, tap Ignore and the warning goes away in that bed.

Dates come from your last spring frost and first fall frost in Settings. Change them and every date moves with them. Mark each step as you do it, and later steps are counted from the day you actually did it.

After you mark a square harvested, Next in this square lists crops that can still be ready before fall frost. Tap one to plant it.

Start the next season from Seasons. Last season's beds stay there, read only, and a yellow corner marks squares where the same family grew last year.

Spacing, dates and neighbor notes are a general guide. Your local conditions come first.

To edit or delete a bed, use the edit button on the bed page, or touch and hold the bed on the home screen.
