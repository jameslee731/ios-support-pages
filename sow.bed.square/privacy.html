Privacy Policy

Four by Eight Privacy

Four by Eight stores your beds, the crops in each square, your frost dates, the steps you mark and any harvest notes on this iPhone. There is no account and no sign-in.

Bed images are created on this iPhone. They leave the phone only when you share them through the system share sheet, to the place you choose.

Four by Eight does not use your location, camera, photos or contacts.

Four by Eight does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not show ads.

Deleting a bed, using Delete all beds in About, or deleting the app removes the beds and seasons stored by Four by Eight.
