Slag Line

Slag Line shows how much working lining is left, which stations to gun, and whether the next heat can be charged.

The shop

Load the example ladle, or add the ladle in front of you. The map is the latest heat. Slag line, barrel, and bottom each have eight stations. Amber means gun that station. Red means tear the lining out.

A station

Tap a station and drag the inner face to the thickness you measured. Save this reading. When the decision is Gun, mark the patch. The saved thickness does not change.

The next heat

Charge the next heat stays off while a station must be torn out, or while a station still needs a patch. After the patch is marked, charge copies the patched thickness into a new heat.

Two linings

Compare shows this lining and the last one, in millimeters per heat. A torn-out lining is not edited there.
