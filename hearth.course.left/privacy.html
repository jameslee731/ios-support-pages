Privacy Policy

Slag Line keeps the residual thickness of one ladle and the decision for the next heat. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The ladles, the heat readings, and the patches you mark are stored on this iPhone. Slag Line does not ask you to create an account.

What Slag Line does not collect

Slag Line does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.
