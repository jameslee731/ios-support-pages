Privacy Policy

Cork Board Privacy

Cork Board stores the notes, photos, recordings, and transcripts you pin on this iPhone. There is no account.

Photos are chosen only to pin one piece, or to place one picture under the wall. The microphone records a voice note. Speech is turned into text on this iPhone so that note can be searched.

Sharing sends only what you choose to share, through the system share sheet.

Deleting the app deletes what Cork Board stored.
