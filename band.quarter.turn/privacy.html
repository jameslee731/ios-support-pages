Privacy Policy

Cardweave is a tablet-weaving planner. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The bands you design, the pick you have woven to, and the lengths you enter for yarn are stored on this iPhone. Cardweave does not ask you to create an account.

Files you share

You can share one repeat of a band as a PDF or a picture through the share sheet. Cardweave does not upload those files. The place they go is the app or person you choose.

What Cardweave does not collect

Cardweave does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
