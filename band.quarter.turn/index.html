Cardweave

Cardweave helps you design a tablet-woven band, follow each pick, and write a warp slip for the yarn.

Design a band

Open a sample band or start a new one. Thread the four holes of each card, set the slant, and arrange forward and back turns into packs. The loom shows one repeat of the band and marks a pack that needs to turn the other way.

Weave

The weave screen moves one pick at a time and tells you which way the cards turn. You can step back one pick. Closing the app keeps the pick you reached.

Yarn

Enter the finished length, the fringe, and the loom waste. Cardweave writes how many threads to cut and how much weft the band needs.

Share

From the band you have open, share one repeat as a PDF or a picture.
