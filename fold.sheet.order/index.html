Foldorder

Foldorder shows which pages print on the front and back of each sheet, so you can fold a short booklet without copying a page table from a website.

Tap New job. Name the booklet, type the finished page count, and choose A4 or Letter and saddle fold or single leaf. If the count is not a multiple of four, Foldorder pads it and writes the padded count on the sheet.

Each screen is one piece of paper. The numbers are the pages that print on this side. Flip to the back of the same sheet. Open Folded order to check reading order, then stamp Folded or Sewn.

Try a 16-page sample on an empty home screen if you want a job already laid out. Export schematic shares a PDF of every sheet through the system share sheet. Foldorder does not send pages to a printer.

Jobs stay on this iPhone until you delete them. Delete all jobs is in About.
