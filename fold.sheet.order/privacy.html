Foldorder Privacy

Foldorder stores the jobs you create: the name, page count, paper size, binding, sheet page numbers, and whether each sheet is unprinted, folded, or sewn. There is no account and no sign-in.

That data stays on this iPhone. Foldorder does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not show ads.

Foldorder does not use the camera, photo library, location, microphone or contacts.

A schematic PDF leaves the phone only when you tap Export schematic and choose a destination in the system share sheet.

Deleting a job, Delete all jobs in About, or deleting the app, removes the jobs stored by Foldorder.
