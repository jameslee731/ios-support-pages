Coverlabel Privacy

Coverlabel stores the diagrams you keep: the name, the photo copy, each cover and its answer, and when that cover is due again. There is no account and no sign-in.

That data stays on this iPhone. Coverlabel does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not show ads.

The camera is used only after you tap Take a photo, so this one diagram can be copied into Coverlabel. Choosing a photo uses the system photo picker and does not read the rest of your library. Coverlabel does not use the microphone, location, or contacts.

Coverlabel reads the printed words on a photo you added, on this phone, so it can place a cover on each label. Your photos and answers are not uploaded.

Deleting a diagram, Delete all diagrams in About, or deleting the app, removes the diagrams stored by Coverlabel.
