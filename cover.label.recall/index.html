Coverlabel

Coverlabel covers the labels on a diagram you photographed and asks you to name what is underneath.

Tap Try a sample diagram on an empty home screen. It is a drawing of the heart with eight labels, all due today. Tap Start review, say the name, then tap Show answer. Got it waits longer. Missed comes back tomorrow.

To use your own page, tap Add. Take a photo or choose one photo. Check the covers, fix any answer, or draw a cover where a label was missed. Save the diagram and start a review.

Trouble spots are labels you have missed more than once. Drill trouble spots reviews only those.

Diagrams stay on this iPhone until you delete them. Delete all diagrams is in About. Coverlabel does not upload the photo or the answers.
