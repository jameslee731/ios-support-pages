Privacy Policy

Ownhand Privacy

Ownhand stores the fonts you make: the name, the pen, the spacing, and the strokes for each letter. There is no account and no sign-in.

That data stays on this iPhone. Ownhand does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not show ads.

Ownhand does not use the camera, microphone, location, or contacts. A card picture is written only when you export one. Saving it to Photos is a choice in the system share sheet.

Deleting a font, Delete all fonts in About, or deleting the app, removes the fonts stored by Ownhand.
