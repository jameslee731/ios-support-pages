Privacy Policy

Ladderfit Privacy

Ladderfit stores the gels you create, the photos you add to them, the well line, ladder marks, sample marks and the sizes it reads on this iPhone. There is no account and no sign-in.

Photos you add are copied into Ladderfit. Your photo library is never changed, and Ladderfit does not scan the library. Copies leave the phone only when you export a marked gel or a CSV through the system share sheet, to the place you choose.

Ladderfit uses the camera only when you tap Take photo, so it can photograph the gel on your bench. You can refuse camera access and still choose a photo already on this phone, or use a gel you already added.

Ladderfit does not use your location, microphone or contacts.

Ladderfit does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not show ads.

Deleting a gel, Delete all gels in About, or deleting the app, removes the copies stored by Ladderfit.
