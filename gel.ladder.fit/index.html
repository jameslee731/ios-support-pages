Ladderfit

Ladderfit helps you photograph a gel, mark the ladder, tap the bands, and read bp or kDa off the same photo.

Tap New gel, give the run a name and choose bp or kDa. Photograph the gel on the bench, or choose a photo already on this phone. Photos are copied into Ladderfit; the originals are not changed.

On Mark, drag the amber line to the wells. Switch to Ladder and tap each known band, then type its size. Three marks are enough to read. Switch to Bands and tap the sample bands. Reads appear on the gel.

Read sizes shows the log-linear fit, R², and a table of every sample. Export a marked PNG of the gel, or a CSV of the sizes. Sharing opens the system share sheet only after you tap it.

Try a sample gel on the empty home screen loads a 16S PCR gel that is already marked, so you can read sizes without taking a photo.

Reads are estimates from the ladder you marked, not a lab instrument. Gels, photos and marks stay on this iPhone. Delete all gels is in About.
