Crossknit

Crossknit turns your own words and clues into a numbered crossword.

Type each answer with a clue, or import a CSV with two columns: word, clue. Tap Build grid. Shuffle for another layout, lock a word to keep it in place, and solve on the phone or export an A4 or US Letter PDF with a separate answer page.

Answers use the letters A to Z. Spaces, hyphens and apostrophes are dropped from the grid and shown in the clue, for example (3,5) for ICE CREAM. A puzzle takes 2 to 30 words of 2 to 15 letters.

If a word is listed under Didn't fit, it shares no letters with the rest or every crossing would touch another word. Change the word, add a word that shares its letters, or try Shuffle.
