Crossknit Privacy

Crossknit stores your word lists, clues, grids and solving progress on this iPhone. There is no account and no sign-in.

If you import a CSV file, Crossknit reads only the file you pick and copies its words and clues into a puzzle.

Exporting a PDF creates the file on this iPhone. It leaves the phone only when you share it through the system share sheet, to the place you choose.

Crossknit does not collect personal data, does not use analytics, and does not show ads.

Deleting a puzzle, using Delete all puzzles in About, or deleting the app removes the puzzles stored by Crossknit.
