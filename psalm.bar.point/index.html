Barpoint

Barpoint points a psalm or canticle, then lets you hear soprano, alto, tenor, and bass before rehearsal.

Point

Open a sample service, or add a psalm, a canticle, or text you paste with a colon between the halves. The bars follow the speech rhythm. Tap a gap between syllables to move the nearest bar. A word outside the built-in list is marked so you can check it.

Sing

Sing plays the four parts for the verse on screen. You can solo one part, change the tempo, or shift the key. The tunes are original desk chants written for Barpoint.

Share

From the service you have open, share one pointed page as a PDF.
