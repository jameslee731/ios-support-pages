Privacy Policy

Barpoint points psalms and canticles for Anglican chant, then plays the four parts. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The services you create, the chant you pick, and any bars you move are stored on this iPhone. Barpoint does not ask you to create an account.

Files you share

You can share one pointed page as a PDF through the share sheet. Barpoint does not upload that file. The place it goes is the app or person you choose.

What Barpoint does not collect

Barpoint does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text replaces this page.
