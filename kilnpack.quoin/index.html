Kiln Load

Kiln Load shows which pots touch, which hang off the shelf, and which corner post is carrying too much.

A firing

Add a firing, or open one that is already loaded. Each piece is a circle. Drag it. Its weight goes to the nearest of the four corner posts.

The shelf

A red circle is touching another piece, or it hangs off the shelf. A red post is over the limit you set. The load on each post is the sum of the pieces that sit nearest to it.

A piece

Tap a circle to set its name, radius, and weight. Keep piece writes that back onto the shelf.
