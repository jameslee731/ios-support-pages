Privacy Policy

Kiln Load shows how a shelf of pots sits before a firing. It runs on your iPhone.

What stays on this phone

The firings, the pieces, and where you drag them are stored on this iPhone. Kiln Load does not ask you to create an account.

What Kiln Load does not collect

Kiln Load does not collect your name, contacts, location, or how you use the app. There is no advertising in the app and nothing to buy.
